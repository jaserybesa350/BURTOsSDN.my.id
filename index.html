Our Organizational Mission

Fostering Long-term Social Mobility through Targeted Support

Founded in New York in 2013, BURTON FOUNDATION was established to bridge systemic gaps in educational access, social support networks, and community enrichment. We work alongside grassroots non-profits, academic institutions, and civic organizations to deliver sustainable, direct-impact solutions.

Strategic Philanthropy

Our Core Focus Areas

We concentrate our efforts on three primary pillars designed to maximize measurable community impact and create generational change.

Pillar I

Youth & Education Grants

Supporting after-school enrichment programs, STEM learning kits, and higher education access for underprivileged students in urban communities.

Pillar II

Community Welfare & Relief

Funding local food security initiatives, housing assistance support, and emergency neighborhood revitalization funds across New York.

Pillar III

Civic Engagement & Arts

Championing public arts projects, cultural preservation, and civic advocacy workshops that foster inclusivity and creative expression.

Grant Application Cycle 2026-2027

Applying for a Burton Foundation Grant

We accept grant proposals from qualified 501(c)(3) public charities, non-profit community groups, and educational organizations on a rolling quarterly basis.

Quarterly Board Review Applications reviewed in March, June, September, and December.
Grant Ranges Micro-grants from $5,000 to Major Program Grants up to $150,000.

4-Step Application Process

1

3
Full Proposal Invitation

4
Award & Disbursal

Board approval and grant release.
